Third-party 3D assets used in this scene
=========================================

The scene commits to a single visual family: Kenney flat-shaded CC0
low-poly. Every downloaded GLB below ships under CC0 1.0 Universal
(public domain, no attribution required).

The old "floating-islands-with-tables" layout was retired and replaced
by an outdoor exposition park — continuous grass ground, stone paths
connecting six ground-level zones (Hub / Gallery / Projects / Skills /
Experience / Contact). Every prop in every zone is a Kenney GLB;
procedural arches / posters / plinths were removed wholesale.

kenney/ (Nature Kit)           — https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit
  tree_oak, tree_fat, plant_bushDetailed, plant_bushLarge,
  flower_yellowA, flower_purpleA, flower_redA, mushroom_red,
  mushroom_tanGroup, campfire_stones, rock_largeA, log_stack,
  ground_grass, ground_pathOpen, ground_pathCorner, ground_pathCross,
  ground_pathEnd, ground_pathBend
  Usage: ground grass, path tiles, scatter flora across the park and
  inside each zone.

kenney/ (Castle Kit)           — https://kenney.nl/assets/castle-kit
  tower-square-base, tower-square-base-border, tower-square-arch,
  tower-hexagon-base, tower-hexagon-mid, tower-hexagon-top,
  flag-pennant, rocks-large, rocks-small, gate, stairs-stone,
  ground-hills
  Usage: Hub welcome gate, Experience monoliths (hex-tower stacks
  with pennants), Gallery sculptures, Contact info booth.

kenney/ (Furniture Kit)        — https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit
  bench, benchCushion, lampRoundFloor, lampRoundTable,
  plantSmall1, plantSmall2, plantSmall3
  Usage: benches + lamps around Hub and Contact, potted plants.

kenney/ (Holiday Kit)          — https://kenney.nl/assets/holiday-kit
  lantern, lantern-hanging
  Usage: Contact lanterns flanking the info booth.

kenney/ (Space Kit)            — https://kenney.nl/assets/space-kit
  satelliteDish_detailed, astronautA, craft_speederA, meteor_detailed
  Usage: satelliteDish_detailed — Skills "AI / LLM" skill monument.
         astronautA — Hub companion next to the mascot.
         craft_speederA — ambient flyer orbiting the scene.

kenney/ (Mini-Arcade)          — https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-arcade
  arcade-machine, pinball, ticket-machine
  Usage: Three Projects kiosks (one per project).

robot-expressive.glb
  Source  : three.js examples — RobotExpressive
  URL     : https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/master/examples/models/gltf/RobotExpressive/
  License : MIT (three.js repo)
  Notes   : The mascot. 13 rigged animation clips used across the gesture
            palette. Kept for its clips; closely matches the Kenney toy
            aesthetic.
